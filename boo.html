Wonderland Playhouse • Closed 1998

The rusted padlock on Wonderland Playhouse's side door snapped off three winters ago. You slipped inside after midnight to retrieve your younger brother's lost windbreaker from the prize counter—only for the steel shutter behind Reception to drop and bolt itself shut.


On the damp carpet beside the turnstile sits a heavy, scarred red rubber play-ball stamped PROPERTY OF WONDERLAND. Whenever you throw it down a hall, no matter the angle, it always finds a way back to your feet.


Recover the three Brass Exit Tokens to unlatch the Reception Shutter—before the building finishes resetting for the night.
